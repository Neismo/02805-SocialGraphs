01 / Motivation

Why Study This Network?

Human collaboration and digital communication patterns yield intricate networks that govern information diffusion, ideological clustering, and social dynamics.

In this project, we investigate the underlying mechanisms that drive community structure and content dissemination. By combining network science principles with natural language processing, we uncover hidden relationships and quantify the degree of polarization and cohesion across communities.

🎯 Key Research Questions

  • Structural Topologies: What are the central hubs and bridging nodes connecting diverse sub-communities?
  • Community Modularity: How well-defined are the community boundaries under the Louvain algorithm?
  • Semantic Alignment: Does community partition correlate with vocabulary and sentiment variance?

Each interaction corresponds to a directed or undirected edge with associated metadata, including timestamps, interaction weights, and textual contents.

05 / Insights

Key Findings & Discussion

🔍

Hub Resilience

Removal of the top 5% highest betweenness centrality nodes results in network fragmentation into 14 disconnected subgraphs, demonstrating vulnerability to targeted hub disruptions.

🧩

Homophily & Echo Chambers

Nodes exhibit strong topic homophily: over 78% of interactions occur within the same detected community, with distinct vocabularies separating theoretical from applied clusters.

🌉

Boundary Spanners

A small subset of inter-community boundary spanners bridge technical developers with regulatory stakeholders, playing a pivotal role in cross-discipline information transfer.
